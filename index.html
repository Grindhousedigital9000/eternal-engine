If nothing could ever end, could anything ever truly begin?

The Eternal Engine has turned against the world, and every fallen champion it brings back to life. Choose a hero, step up to a tabletop battlefield in front of you, and fight through endless hordes as your weapons fire on their own. Level up, combine weapons and runes into powerful evolutions, and push deeper toward the heart of the Engine to break the cycle.

10heroes, each with their own starting weapon
60weapons and 60 evolutions
6stages, from the Forgotten Battleground to the Heart of the Engine
55enemies and 6 bosses

Features

  • A survivor roguelite you play standing or seated, looking down on a living tabletop battlefield.
  • Move with the thumbstick; your weapons attack automatically. Hundreds of enemies on screen at once.
  • Level-up choices, chests, shrines and rerolls make every run different.
  • Recover lost memories, unlock heroes and upgrades in the Sanctuary, and discover three endings.
  • Adjust the table's height, size, tilt and distance so it fits your space.
  • Fully offline. No accounts, no ads, no data collected.
